Welcome to the Avatar Legends Wiki!

Water. Earth. Fire. Air. Master the elements and clash as legendary characters in Avatar Legends: The Fighting Game. Featuring hand-drawn 2D animation faithful to the series’ roots, the game lets players experience the world of Avatar through a variety of modes and features. Choose from 12 playable characters and compete across modes including Offline and Online Versus, Story, Arcade, Training, and Spectator, alongside an Art Gallery celebrating the history of the series.
